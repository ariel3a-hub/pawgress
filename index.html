Dog care, together

Everyone caring for the dog, in one timeline.

Sign up, create or join a group, add your dogs, and log every fed, walked, pooped and peed event. Everyone who cares for that dog sees what happened and who did it.

What it does

🐕 Dogs

Name, birthday and photo (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 5 MB). Managed by the group owner.

📋 Event log

Press a dog's photo, then press an event. Entries read like "Walked with ariel".

🗓️ Timeline

The full history grouped by day, with a dropdown to switch between dogs when there is more than one.

👥 Groups

Create a group or join one with a 6-character code. Every member can log events.

🔔 Reminders

Any member schedules a reminder and it raises a notification for everyone in the group when it comes due.

🔐 Accounts

Sign up with username, email and password. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256.

Under the hood

Pawgress is built with FastAPI, server-rendered Jinja2 templates, SQLAlchemy and SQLite. It uses server-side sessions and signed cookies, and it supports optional Web Push for system notifications when the tab is closed.

Notifications are configurable: toggle them per event type, so you only hear about the things you actually care about.
